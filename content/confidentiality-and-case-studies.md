Client names stay private. Renegade Penguin LLC does not publish client lists, customer logos, identifying quotes, or engagement details that could reasonably identify a client.

Public case studies are handled in one of two ways. Most are anonymized composites built from recurring patterns across different kinds of work. A composite does not describe one client, one incident, or one environment. Details are changed, combined, or removed so the example can explain the work without exposing who received it.

A case study about a specific engagement is published only with the client’s written sign-off. Even with permission, unnecessary identifying details, internal system names, sensitive findings, and security configurations are removed. Approval for one case study does not grant permission to use the client’s name or logo elsewhere.

Pro bono work receives the same confidentiality treatment as paid work. The absence of a fee does not make the client, recipient, or work public. No organization, faith, community, or other group is identified without explicit written permission.

Instead of client claims or logo walls, this site may show:

- Sample scopes of work
- Redacted or reconstructed deliverable formats
- Anonymized composite scenarios
- Example review questions and decision records
- Plain explanations of the methods used

These materials are examples, not claims about a particular client or a promise that every engagement will produce the same result.

If a public example could reveal more than it teaches, it does not get published.
