Security and IT decisions need an owner, even when hiring a full-time security or technology executive does not make sense. This advisory retainer provides vCISO- and vCIO-style support focused on clear priorities, defensible decisions, and work your organization can sustain.

## Who it is for

This service fits small and midsize organizations that need regular access to experienced security and IT guidance. It also works for leadership teams dealing with growth, vendor changes, customer security questions, or responsibilities that have outgrown an informal approach.

## What is included

Advisory work may include:

- Reviewing your current security posture and building a practical roadmap
- Defining policy, risk ownership, and decision-making responsibilities
- Evaluating vendors and tools with no sacred cows
- Preparing for incidents and clarifying who does what
- Translating technical risk into useful leadership reporting
- Helping internal IT teams set priorities and challenge assumptions
- Reviewing plans, proposals, contracts, and security questionnaires

The work is defense first and vendor-neutral. Recommendations are based on your actual risks, constraints, and operating needs.

## What is not included

An advisory retainer is not a managed security service, help desk, outsourced IT department, penetration test, legal opinion, or assurance against incidents. Hands-on engineering can be scoped as a separate project when needed.

## How it starts

We begin with a scoping call to understand the organization, its decision-makers, and the problems that need attention first. From there, we agree on access, communication, priorities, and a working rhythm.

The retainer is month to month with no lock-in. If it stops being useful, it should stop.
