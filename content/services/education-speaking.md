Security education should help people make better decisions when the workshop is over. It should not depend on fear, product pitches, or a wall of unexplained terminology.

Renegade Penguin offers workshops, talks, and facilitated sessions for technical teams, leadership groups, community organizations, and event audiences. Material can be adapted to the audience’s existing knowledge and the decisions they need to make.

Topics include:

- Open-source intelligence, or OSINT
- Operational security, or OPSEC
- Threat modeling for systems, organizations, and individuals
- Defensive security fundamentals
- Incident readiness and decision-making
- Vendor claims, security tradeoffs, and better questions to ask
- Practical security for small organizations with limited staff

Sessions can range from an introductory talk to a working session built around a real system or scenario. Workshops may include exercises, discussion prompts, reference material, and a written summary of decisions or next steps.

The goal is understanding, not performance. Technical concepts are explained in plain language without assuming the audience already knows the vocabulary, and technical audiences still get the detail needed to challenge the material.

Education and speaking do not replace a security assessment, legal advice, or hands-on engineering. If a session uncovers a larger issue, any follow-up work is scoped separately.
