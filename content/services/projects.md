Some security work needs advice. Other work needs someone to inspect the system, change the configuration, document the result, and leave it in better shape.

Projects are scoped around a specific problem and a defined set of deliverables. The work is defense first, based on evidence, and designed to fit the people who will maintain it after the engagement ends.

## Defensive engineering and hardening

Projects may include:

- Identity, access, and administrative privilege reviews
- Endpoint, server, network, cloud, and SaaS hardening
- Logging, alerting, backup, and recovery improvements
- Secure configuration baselines
- Incident readiness and response playbooks
- Architecture and data-flow reviews
- Policy implementation tied to actual technical controls
- Security roadmaps aligned to the NIST CSF, CIS Controls, ISO 27001, or HIPAA Security Rule

Deliverables depend on scope and may include findings, prioritized recommendations, configuration changes, diagrams, runbooks, decision records, or a handoff session with the people responsible for ongoing operations.

## AI security review

The fixed-scope AI security review looks at how AI is actually being used, including use that never went through a formal approval process.

The review covers:

- Acceptable-use expectations
- A shadow AI inventory
- Data flows and sensitive-data exposure
- Access, retention, and training questions
- Vendor claims and contract questions
- Practical controls for approved use

The result is a documented view of current use, material risks, open questions, and prioritized next steps.

## Scope and exclusions

Each project has written boundaries, assumptions, client responsibilities, and acceptance criteria. Penetration testing, legal advice, continuous monitoring, managed detection and response, and general help-desk work are excluded unless specifically stated.

When specialist work is needed, trusted experts in areas such as penetration testing or telecom can be brought in as a coordinated team. That involvement is disclosed and agreed on before work begins.

Projects have a minimum fee and are quoted after a scoping call.
