Renegade Penguin LLC helps organizations understand risk, improve defenses, and make sound technology decisions without loyalty to any vendor or product.

## Cybersecurity Posture and Direction

I assess where an organization stands, identify meaningful gaps, and help leadership decide where the organization can and should go. The result is clear direction based on the organization’s needs, resources, and risks.

There are no sacred cows. Existing vendors, products, and approaches remain only when they serve the work.

## Defensive Engineering

Defense comes first. I help design and improve systems that must withstand real attacks. Friends and associates who work on the offensive side can test that work and challenge its assumptions.

## AI Security

I help organizations examine the security risks created by AI use and determine practical safeguards for their environment.

## Education and Speaking

Education is available in:

- Open source intelligence, or OSINT
- Operational security, or OPSEC
- Threat modeling

I also speak on cybersecurity, IT, and related security practices.

## Specialist Teams

Renegade Penguin is one-person-led. When an engagement requires additional expertise, we form a team from a trusted network of specialists. This can include telecommunications support for call centers and penetration testing.

The team fits the engagement. It does not add complexity for its own sake.
