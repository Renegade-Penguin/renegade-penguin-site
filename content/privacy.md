Renegade Penguin LLC operates a static website hosted on Cloudflare Pages. The site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party requests on ordinary pages.

## What is collected

If you only read the site, Renegade Penguin does not ask you to provide personal information. Cloudflare may create ordinary server logs when it delivers a page. Those logs may include information such as an IP address, request time, requested page, browser details, and security events.

The contact page loads one Cloudflare Turnstile script to help block automated spam. When you submit the contact form, Renegade Penguin receives the information you enter:

- Name
- Work email
- Organization
- Organization size band
- What you need
- Budget band
- Timeline
- How you heard about Renegade Penguin
- Consent to be contacted

Do not submit passwords, private keys, malware, regulated data, or sensitive evidence through the form.

## Why it is collected

Contact form information is used to review your request, respond to you, assess whether Renegade Penguin may be able to help, scope possible work, and keep reasonable business records. Turnstile and ordinary server logs are used to deliver and protect the site, reduce spam, and investigate technical or security problems.

Sending the form does not create an advisory relationship.

## Who processes it

Cloudflare hosts the site, provides Turnstile, runs the Worker that handles submissions, and maintains ordinary server logs. The Worker sends form submissions to contact@renegadepenguinllc.com, where the mailbox provider processes the message.

Renegade Penguin does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be disclosed when required by law, needed to protect the site or its users, or necessary for service providers to operate the site and mailbox.

## Retention and deletion

Contact information is kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, evaluate or perform work, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Cloudflare controls retention of its ordinary server logs under its own practices.

To ask what contact information Renegade Penguin has about you, correct it, or request deletion, email [contact@renegadepenguinllc.com](mailto:contact@renegadepenguinllc.com). Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or legitimate business reasons.
