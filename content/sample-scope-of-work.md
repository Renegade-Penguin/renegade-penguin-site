# Sample Only

This page is an example, not an offer or a binding agreement. The final statement of work will reflect the client’s environment, priorities, and written agreement.

## Purpose

Renegade Penguin LLC will provide independent cybersecurity and IT advice to help the client evaluate decisions, prioritize defensive work, and review implementation plans. The engagement begins with a one-time assessment so ongoing advice is based on the client’s actual environment.

## Deliverables

The engagement may include:

- An initial assessment summary and prioritized action plan
- Review of security architecture, policies, controls, or vendor proposals
- Written recommendations with assumptions and dependencies identified
- Decision notes from scheduled working sessions
- A maintained list of open questions, risks, and next actions
- Periodic review of progress against agreed priorities

Deliverables are advisory unless implementation work is added to the scope in writing.

## Cadence

The parties will hold a working session on an agreed weekly or twice-monthly schedule. Renegade Penguin will provide written follow-up notes after each session and respond to reasonable questions between meetings.

Unused time does not automatically carry forward unless the final statement of work says otherwise.

## Assumptions

The client will provide timely access to relevant people, documents, and systems. Recommendations will be based on the information available during the engagement. Material changes to the environment or requested work may require a revised scope.

## Exclusions

Unless added in writing, the engagement excludes continuous monitoring, managed security services, emergency incident response, legal advice, compliance certification, penetration testing, and operation of client production systems.

## Client Responsibilities

The client will:

- Name an authorized engagement contact
- Provide accurate and complete information
- Maintain backups and change-control procedures
- Review recommendations and approve business decisions
- Control access to its systems and third-party services

## Term and Ending the Engagement

After the assessment, the retainer continues month to month with no long-term lock-in. Either party may end it under the notice terms in the final written agreement.

At the end, Renegade Penguin will provide current handover notes, identify unresolved items, remove or return access, and delete client data on request.
