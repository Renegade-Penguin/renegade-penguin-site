Renegade Penguin LLC aims to align this website with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

The site uses a dark, high-contrast design and is intended to support keyboard navigation, text resizing, and common assistive technology. Information should not depend on color alone. The site does not use animation or motion effects as part of its content or navigation.

Accessibility is ongoing work, and there may be barriers that have not yet been found. Content linked from other websites may also have accessibility limits outside Renegade Penguin’s control. Differences among browsers, operating systems, assistive technologies, and user settings can affect how a page works.

If something prevents you from reading the site, navigating it, or using the contact form, email [contact@renegadepenguinllc.com](mailto:contact@renegadepenguinllc.com). Please include:

- The page or feature that caused the problem
- What you were trying to do
- Your browser and assistive technology, if you are comfortable sharing them
- A description of what happened

You should receive a response within 5 business days. Renegade Penguin will review the barrier and provide the information in another reasonable format when possible.
