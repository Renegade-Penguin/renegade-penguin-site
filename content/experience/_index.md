The work spans environments where technology, safety, privacy, and daily operations meet.

## Experience Across Different Environments

I have worked across medical, dental, private aviation, critical infrastructure, and managed service provider settings supporting small businesses. Each environment has different constraints, but the basic requirement remains the same: understand what matters, identify credible risks, and improve defenses without losing sight of operations.

## A Warning That Went Unheard

Years ago, I gave an organization a clear warning about serious security weaknesses. It was ignored until the cost became unavoidable.

I did not take that as proof that I was right. I took it as a reminder that improving security starts with listening.

## Tested Assumptions

My approach is defense first. Defensive work should still face serious scrutiny, so friends and associates on the offensive side test it against real attacks.

When an engagement needs skills beyond my own, trusted specialists join the team. That may include penetration testing or telecommunications expertise for call centers. The purpose is to bring the right experience to the problem while keeping responsibility and direction clear.
