I’m ChiefGyk3D, the independent security advisor behind Renegade Penguin LLC. I have about 16 years in the field, with work across private aviation, critical infrastructure, cryptocurrency, and managed services for healthcare, legal, and dental settings.

I architect security best practices, help developers shift left, build secure programs, and help organizations define initial security requirements and vet early security hires.

I use open source and proprietary technology, then recommend based on results. Renegade Penguin does not resell products or accept referral fees. I also build in the open on GitHub.

My credentials and more of my work are available on [my personal site, chiefgyk3d.com](https://chiefgyk3d.com/).

I have spent about 16 years working in the field, learning security by building, testing, breaking, and understanding the systems involved. My networking background reaches down to the radio layer, and my experience in emergency communications taught me to treat continuity and disaster recovery as operational work that must survive real failure.

I have seen both the defensive and underground sides of security, and I know Tor and I2P beyond the usual boogeyman stories. I research and build heavily in open source, track work such as Veilid and post-quantum cryptography, and explain complex subjects without stripping away the details that matter. I will not ask someone else to do work I have not tried to understand myself, and I bring that same standard to executive claims: clear questions, technical evidence, and no fluff.

Bring the hard questions to a call, and I will answer them directly.

I started in networking, Linux and system administration, then moved into cybersecurity. Linux remains my daily driver, and I have used it in production alongside hands-on work in networking architecture, identity, detection and response, cloud security and automation.

That systems background affects how I approach security work. I want to understand how something is built, who operates it and what can realistically be maintained before I recommend controls around it.

Renegade Penguin is based in Ohio and works with organizations across Ohio and the United States. Ohio organizations can read about the state's cybersecurity framework affirmative defense on the [Ohio guidance page](/ohio/).
