## Have we completed a useful risk analysis?

A risk analysis for the HIPAA Security Rule should describe how electronic protected health information moves through the practice, where it is stored, who can reach it, and what could affect its confidentiality, integrity, or availability. It should lead to decisions and documented follow-through, not sit untouched after completion.

Renegade Penguin can assess current safeguards, identify gaps, and help build a practical improvement plan aligned to the HIPAA Security Rule. There is no HIPAA certification, and no consultant can issue one.

## What should we ask vendors and business associates?

Practices depend on EHR providers, imaging vendors, billing services, cloud platforms, managed service providers, and other outside organizations. The useful questions are specific: What information can the vendor access? How is that access limited? What happens when staff leave? How are incidents reported? Can data be recovered?

A business associate agreement may be requested where appropriate. Renegade Penguin will sign a business associate agreement when the work involves protected health information, subject to case-by-case review before work begins.

## Are we ready for ransomware or another disruptive incident?

Readiness includes more than endpoint software. A practice should know whether backups can be restored, which systems must return first, how downtime procedures work, and who can make decisions when clinical or administrative systems are unavailable.

EHR and imaging systems need particular care because availability matters and changes may depend on vendors, specialized equipment, or support agreements. Recommendations account for those constraints instead of treating every device like an ordinary office computer.

## Is staff training relevant to the way we work?

Training should match the actual work. Front-desk staff, clinicians, billing personnel, administrators, and outside support providers do not face identical decisions. Clear reporting procedures and short, relevant exercises are more useful than generic annual slides alone.
