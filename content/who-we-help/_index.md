Security decisions look different depending on what you operate, what information you hold, and what cannot stop working.

- [Small businesses](/who-we-help/small-business/) get practical guidance without hiring a full-time security leader.
- [Medical and dental practices](/who-we-help/healthcare-dental/) get help with risk analysis, vendors, systems, training, and incident readiness.
- [Private aviation organizations](/who-we-help/aviation/) get advice grounded in the systems, people, and vendors that keep operations moving.
- [Critical infrastructure organizations](/who-we-help/critical-infrastructure/) get careful support for operational technology, segmentation, and safe change.

Review the available [services](/services/), or [contact Renegade Penguin](/contact/) to discuss what fits your organization.
