## Which systems keep the operation moving?

Private aviation depends on scheduling platforms, dispatch workflows, maintenance records, payment systems, communications, and access to facilities. The first step is identifying which systems matter most, who can access them, and what the operation would do if one became unavailable.

## How are passenger and crew data protected?

Passenger details, crew records, identification documents, travel plans, and payment information may move among internal staff, aircraft owners, service providers, and software platforms. Renegade Penguin can map those paths, review access, and identify places where sensitive information is retained or shared without a clear need.

## Where do aircraft and ground systems fit?

Aircraft systems, maintenance equipment, fuel systems, cameras, building controls, wireless networks, and ordinary business IT have different operating constraints. Good security separates them where practical and avoids changes that could interrupt safety-sensitive or vendor-supported systems.

## What risk comes through vendors?

Scheduling, maintenance, avionics, connectivity, payment, and support vendors may hold data or maintain remote access. Reviews focus on what each vendor can reach, how access is controlled, and what happens during an outage or incident.
