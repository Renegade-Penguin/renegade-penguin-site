## Confidentiality and NDAs

Client work is confidential by default. Renegade Penguin LLC does not name clients, display client logos, or disclose engagement details without written permission.

Client-provided nondisclosure agreements can be reviewed before sensitive information is exchanged. Any negotiated confidentiality terms must be included in the signed agreement. Confidential information is shared only as needed to perform the work.

## Written Agreements

Every engagement is performed under a written agreement, such as a master services agreement and statement of work or another mutually accepted contract. The agreement defines scope, responsibilities, deliverables, fees, confidentiality terms, and how the engagement may end.

Contract language provided by a client may require review before work begins.

## Healthcare and BAAs

Renegade Penguin’s position on business associate agreements and work involving protected health information is that it will sign a business associate agreement when the work involves protected health information, subject to case-by-case review before work begins.

No protected health information should be provided until the applicable agreement and handling requirements are confirmed in writing.

## Data Handling

Renegade Penguin keeps client data to the minimum needed for the engagement. Collection and retention are limited to information required for the agreed work.

Client data is deleted at the end of the engagement on request, subject to any legal or contractual retention requirement. Sensitive findings and credentials are not placed in public case studies, demonstrations, or training material.

## Access and Devices

MFA is enabled on every account used for client work. Access is requested at the lowest practical level and removed when it is no longer needed.

Client credentials should be issued to named accounts where possible. Shared credentials, standing administrative access, and unnecessary production access are avoided. Devices used for client work receive security updates and use access controls appropriate to the information handled.

## Subcontractors

Renegade Penguin is a one-person practice. No subcontractor performs client work without the client’s written consent. When approved, the subcontractor’s responsibilities and access are limited to the agreed need.

## Insurance

Certificates of insurance are available on request. Insurance limits are not published on this site.

## Security Incidents

Suspected incidents involving client information or access are investigated promptly. If an incident is confirmed to affect a client’s data or systems, the client will be notified according to the written agreement and applicable law. The notification will include the known facts, actions taken, and recommended client steps as that information becomes available.

## Reporting a Site Security Issue

To report a suspected vulnerability or security issue involving this site, follow the instructions in the [security policy](/security-policy/). Please do not include sensitive client information in the initial report.
