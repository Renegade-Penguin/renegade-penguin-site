## What affects a quote

Common factors include system count, environment complexity, access limitations, documentation quality, travel, scheduling, stakeholder availability, reporting needs, and the amount of hands-on implementation required.

Unless a written scope says otherwise, pricing excludes software licenses, hardware, travel expenses, taxes, legal services, penetration testing, continuous monitoring, managed services, emergency response, and work by outside specialists.

Quotes assume the client will provide timely access, accurate information, an authorized decision-maker, and staff who can answer operational questions. Delays, missing access, or material changes to the environment may change the schedule or scope.

A starting range is useful for deciding whether a conversation makes sense. It is not a promise that every environment fits the same shape.
