If you find a security issue affecting this website, email [contact@renegadepenguinllc.com](mailto:contact@renegadepenguinllc.com).

## What to include

Please provide enough detail to reproduce and evaluate the issue:

- The affected page, endpoint, or repository location
- The type of issue and its likely impact
- Clear reproduction steps
- Relevant request and response details
- Any proof of concept that can be shared safely
- Your preferred contact information

Do not include private keys, credentials, personal information, or data belonging to other people. Use encryption before sending sensitive technical details.

## Good-faith research

Renegade Penguin supports good-faith security research. If you make a reasonable effort to avoid privacy violations, service disruption, data destruction, persistence, social engineering, and access beyond what is needed to demonstrate the issue, Renegade Penguin intends not to pursue legal action against you for the research.

Stop testing and report the issue if you encounter sensitive data or gain unintended access. Do not publicly disclose an unresolved issue before there has been a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it.

This policy does not authorize testing of third-party services or infrastructure.

## Response targets

Renegade Penguin aims to acknowledge a report within 2 business days and provide an initial status within 5 business days. Investigation and remediation time will depend on the issue.

There is no bug bounty or promise of payment.

Machine-readable reporting information is available at [/.well-known/security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt).
