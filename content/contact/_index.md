# Contact Renegade Penguin

Start with the problem as you understand it. You do not need to diagnose it, choose a service, or arrive with a finished scope.

## What to include

The contact form asks for:

- Your name
- Your work email
- Your organization
- Your organization’s size band
- What you need help with
- Your budget band, including a “not sure” option
- Your timeline
- How you heard about Renegade Penguin
- Your consent to be contacted about the request

You should receive a response within 2 business days.

## Active security incident

**If you are dealing with an active incident, contact your cyber insurer and law enforcement first. This form is not monitored around the clock and is not an emergency response channel.**

Do not send passwords, private keys, sensitive personal information, regulated data, malware, or detailed evidence through the form. A safer way to exchange material can be established after contact.

## How an engagement starts

The first step is a scoping call to understand the situation, the people involved, the constraints, and what a useful outcome would look like.

Retainer work does not begin on the strength of a sales call. Renegade Penguin completes a one-time assessment before any advisory retainer so both sides have a clear starting point, a practical scope, and enough information to decide whether the relationship fits.

## Privacy

The site uses no cookies or analytics. When the spam check is enabled, the contact page loads one Cloudflare Turnstile script to block spam; otherwise, it loads nothing from Cloudflare. Form submissions are handled by a Cloudflare Pages Function and emailed to contact@renegadepenguinllc.com. Renegade Penguin receives what you enter, and Cloudflare keeps ordinary server logs. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for details.

## No JavaScript?

If the form does not work without JavaScript, email [contact@renegadepenguinllc.com](mailto:contact@renegadepenguinllc.com). Include the same information listed above, but do not email sensitive evidence until a secure exchange method is arranged.
